OUR HISTORY

JCDA Community Center

Where We Began

The Jamaica Community Development Association, also known as JCDA, was founded in 2024 by our Founder and Executive Director, Ms. Edith Torres, with a vision of contributing to the development of Jamaica through meaningful community work. Following her move from Anguilla to Jamaica in 2004, Ms. Torres quickly developed a deep appreciation for the island's beautiful landscapes, welcoming people, rich culture, and unique communities. Inspired by her connection to Jamaica and her belief in the importance of giving back, she was determined to help create a future where Jamaican communities could continue to grow, thrive, and prosper.
